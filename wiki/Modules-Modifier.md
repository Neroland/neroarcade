# Modules: Modifier

The **Modifier** slot adds one twist to the whole game. One chip. Empty means no modifier. Ingredient:
**amethyst shard**. [All slots](Modules.md)

| Chip | What it does | Recipe (shapeless) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fog** | Only a circle around your pawn is visible. | Blank Chip + amethyst shard + gray dye |
| **Low Gravity** | Falling things fall slower; hops last longer. | Blank Chip + amethyst shard + feather |
| **Mirror** | Your controls are flipped on one or both axes. | Blank Chip + amethyst shard + glass |
| **Wrap-around** | The edges join up: leave one side, arrive on the other. | Blank Chip + amethyst shard + string |

## Fog

The screen shows only the cells within the radius of your own pawn. Fog changes what is drawn, not the game itself.

| Setting | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radius (cells) | 5 | 2–12 |

## Low Gravity

Falling Blocks fall more slowly and a Runner's hop lasts longer. At 50 percent, crates fall at half speed and hops
last twice as long.

| Setting | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Percent | 50 | 10–90 |

## Mirror

Movement and aim are flipped on the chosen axis: left is right (Horizontal), up is down (Vertical), or both. The
buttons are not changed.

| Setting | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Axis | Horizontal | Horizontal, Vertical, Both |

## Wrap-around

The board's edges join on the chosen axes, and the border wall on those edges opens. Pawns, hazards and shots that
leave one side come back on the other; Chaser drones take the shorter way round.

| Setting | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Axes | Both | Both, Horizontal, Vertical |

On Ring 24 the left and right edges are always joined; Wrap-around can add the top and bottom.
